Overview

DON’T JUMP is a mobile game. We aim to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to operate and improve the game.

Game progress and settings

Game progress, settings, unlocked content, and locally created gameplay data may be stored on your device so the game can remember your progress and preferences.

Level and challenge sharing

If a version of DON’T JUMP allows you to create or share challenge codes, the code may contain the gameplay information needed to reproduce the level. Do not include personal or sensitive information in custom level names or shared content.

Advertising and third-party services

DON’T JUMP may include optional rewarded advertising, such as an ad offered in exchange for skipping a level. If third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services are included in the released app, those services may process device or usage information according to their own privacy practices and the permissions available on your device.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled in a released version, they may be used to understand general game performance, crashes, feature usage, and level difficulty. This policy will be updated to describe the services and data involved before release.

Children

DON’T JUMP is not designed to require players to create a personal profile or submit personal information in order to play. If the final game is directed to children or includes services that process children’s data, this policy and the app’s privacy controls must be updated accordingly before release.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored only on your device can generally be removed by clearing the game’s data or deleting the app. If future versions store account or online data on a server, this section will be updated with the applicable retention and deletion process.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its features, or its service providers change. The current version will be posted on this page.
