Quick fixes

The game has no sound.

Check the in-game Sound and Music settings, then make sure your device volume is up and Silent Mode is not preventing the audio you expect.

My progress disappeared.

Include your device model and game version when contacting support. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting, because locally stored progress may be removed with the app.

A level seems impossible.

Tell us the level number and what is happening. If something is broken, we want to know. If the level is simply mean, that may be intentional.

I found a bug.

Please send the level number, a short description, and a screen recording if possible.
