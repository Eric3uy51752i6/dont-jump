The spikes are supposed to be mean. The website isn’t. Here are the quickest fixes for common problems.

The game won’t start

  • Use a current version of Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
  • Reload the page once.
  • Disable strict content blockers for this site if the canvas stays blank.
  • Make sure JavaScript and local browser storage are enabled.

No sound or music

Browsers wait for your first tap, click, or key press before allowing audio. Interact with the game, then check the in-game sound setting or the Mute control above the game.

Controls

Desktop: Space, click, or tap to jump. Escape pauses. M toggles sound.

Phone or tablet: Tap the game to jump. Rotate your device whenever you like—the gameplay coordinates remain stable.

Progress and saves

The web version saves progress in local browser storage on the device and browser you use. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or switching browsers may remove or hide that progress.

Browser compatibility

DON’T JUMP is designed for recent versions of Safari, Chrome, Edge, and Firefox on desktop, iPhone, iPad, and Android. Older browsers can run more slowly or omit fullscreen and vibration features.

Something genuinely broke

Email eric@teeter.com. Include your device, browser, level number, and what happened right before the problem.
